    1. 1  THE PUCK

      One board, no soldering

      Watt runs on the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75C: a round touch screen in an aluminium case, with a battery, a speaker and a motion sensor.

      Get the board
    2. 2  THE MAC APP

      Watt lives in the notch

      The app connects the puck to your Mac, and puts Watt on a new puck for you: plug it in, click Install.

      Download for Mac

      macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon. The first release is on its way.

    3. 3  OR FROM HERE

      Install from the browser

      No Mac app yet? Plug the puck in, click the button and pick its port. It takes about a minute, and updates keep the puck paired.

      Open this page in Chrome or Edge Needs a secure (https) page

      Chrome or Edge on a computer.

    4. 4  PAIR

      Dial it in

      Open Watt. When the puck drops out of the notch, click Pair, then turn the puck to each of the three numbers and tap.

      Click the puck's side button any time to pick a face.
