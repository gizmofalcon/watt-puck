Watt, the puck.

A palm-sized dial for your Mac.

Download the app Build the puck

Build it from a $40 board. No soldering.

What it does.

  • Volume and music

    Turn for volume. Tap to pause.

  • Faces

    A timer, a trackpad, Pong. Click the side button to switch.

  • Talk to Claude

    Tap, talk, tap to send.

  • Approve Claude Code

    Tap to allow. No window switching.

Questions.

Can I buy one ready-made?

Not yet. You build it from a $40 board in a few minutes.

Do I need Claude?

No. Volume, music, the timer and the games work on their own.

Which Macs?

Apple silicon, macOS 15 or later.

Is my voice sent anywhere?

English speech becomes text on your Mac. Only the text goes to Claude.
