Watt
Build one

Watt is a palm-sized dial for your Mac. It has a round touchscreen and sits by your keyboard. Turn it for volume, then switch it to a trackpad, a focus timer or Claude.

You build it yourself. It runs on a $40 board, with nothing to solder. The Mac app is free and lives in your notch.

Build one Apple silicon, macOS 15 or later
Watch the demo 3:36

  • Turn for volume. Tap to pause whatever is playing.

  • The side button switches faces. A focus timer, a spinner, dice.

  • Flat on the desk, it's a trackpad. The glass moves your pointer.

  • Lift it up, it's a pointer. Aim at the screen and tap to click.

  • In your dev server, it's a dial. Pick a DialKit control, turn to tune.

  • Talk to Claude. Tap, talk, tap to send.

  • Approve Claude Code. It asks, you answer from the puck.

  • No Mac connected? It plays circular Pong.

Your voice becomes text on your Mac, and only the text goes to Claude. DialKit is by Josh Puckett; to turn its controls from the puck, add dialkit-watt.

Build one. It takes a few minutes once the board arrives.

  1. Get the board. Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75C, about $40.

    Waveshare

  2. Download the app. It finds the board when you plug it in.

    Download

  3. Plug the board in. Click Install in the app. To pair, turn the puck to each number it shows and tap. Or install from this page, in Chrome or Edge. Installing from this page needs https.
