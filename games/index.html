Games for the puck. Open one on your Mac with Watt running, and the puck is the controller. Each one plays with a mouse too.

Torch. A dark room, 36 seconds of light, and something in here with you. Point the puck to search for the key, then the door, and keep an eye on its radar.

Play

Pants. Hills, ramps and springs, with momentum. Hold the puck like a wheel and tilt it to run, tap to jump.

Play

Catch. The puck is the ball. Point it to aim, swipe up to throw, and feel it rock in your hand while it holds what it caught.

Play

Tasks. A small station, six jobs, two and a half minutes. At each one the puck is the panel: turn it, spin it, tap in time, swipe, hold it still, flip it over.

Play

Hamlet. A small village to grow. Swipe on the puck to pick a villager or a building and the map pans to it, turn to choose what it does, tap to do it.

Play

With the puck, the games need Watt open on the same Mac, and a browser that lets this page reach it, such as Chrome.
