0.2.0October 16, 2026

Watt keeps itself and the puck up to date. The app checks for a new version once a day, and sends the puck its updates over Bluetooth.

  • Updates for Watt itself. Check for Updates... is in the menu.
  • Puck updates over Bluetooth. About 30 seconds for a whole firmware.
  • An update that doesn't finish changes nothing. The puck keeps the firmware it had, and goes back to it if the new one can't reconnect to your Mac.
  • Updates wait out calls. While AirPods or another Bluetooth headset is on a call, an update waits until it's over.
  • Enough charge first. The puck takes an update with 25% battery, or on USB power.
  • Firmware 0.2.0. A new flash layout, with room for updates.

Coming from 0.1.0

0.1.0October 2, 2026

The first release. The Mac app, and the firmware it installs on the puck.

  • Home. Volume on the dial, tap to play or pause, and a face that reacts to you.
  • Trackpad and pointer. Flat on the desk, the glass moves your pointer. Lift it up and aim at the screen.
  • Claude. Talk to it from the puck, and allow or deny Claude Code requests.
  • DialKit. Turn real values in the page you're working on, with dialkit-watt.
  • Faces on the side button. Focus timer, Spin, Teams, Dice and Pong.
  • A firmware installer in the app. Plug the board in and click Install.
